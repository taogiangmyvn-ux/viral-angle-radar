# CoBa's Daughter prior-brief patterns

Use these as examples of the brand's working brief format and level of specificity. Campaign-specific requirements must not be copied into unrelated briefs.

## Standard creator-brief structure

1. Open with “Hi lovely!”
2. Name the exact product and deliverable.
3. Put the main reference link near the top.
4. State one clear main idea in plain English.
5. Give a visible content sequence: concern → product use → proof/result → CTA.
6. Separate “Must include” into an approval checklist.
7. Provide natural claim language the creator can say.
8. End with one strong CTA example.
9. Repeat the true approval gates in one final sentence.

Briefs should be concise, creator-friendly, native American English, and executable without a strategy presentation.

## Example 1: Refill Aloe

Product: Refill Aloe pouch + original Aloe jar.

Main reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dak4IbANu-e/

Mandatory droplet reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DaNXox8pcWV/

Main idea: show immediate use after heat, sun, riding, outdoor activity, or a shower. The sequence is:

- Before: skin feels hot, dry, tight, or uncomfortable.
- Use: apply Aloe Gel immediately and show the actual refill.
- After: skin looks calmer, fresher, smoother, and hydrated.

Campaign-specific mandatory shots:

- Refill pouch and original jar
- Actual refill shot
- Clean Aloe droplet hero shot
- Close-up application
- Immediate before-and-after
- Clear product benefit and purchase CTA

Approved wording pattern: Aloe Gel helps cool and soothe hot, dry, or irritated-feeling skin while providing lightweight hydration without feeling heavy or sticky.

CTA pattern: “Grab the limited Aloe before it sells out.”

## Example 2: Endless Summer Amazon Affiliate

Deliverable: 30–60 second vertical summer video in natural sunlight, poolside, or beachside.

Main idea: sell the full-size jar + refill combo. Body care is used generously in summer, so the refill keeps the routine from running out. The emotional shorthand is: the jar stays full, so the summer glow keeps going.

Content sequence:

- Pick one summer skin struggle.
- Show the concern.
- Fix it on camera.
- Show the refill as the reason the routine continues.
- End with glistening, glossy, wet-look skin in sunlight.

Coffee Scrub route:

- Concerns: strawberry legs, KP bumps, rough skin, dead-skin or sunscreen buildup.
- Refill visual: one thick, continuous zig-zag “frosting” pour across the jar.
- Pour reference: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ifjS8zgPvzVRHKjWnyKJjh-O1Vm9Xpz4/view?usp=sharing

Aloe Gel route:

- Concerns: hot, sun-tired skin, visible redness, after-sun cooling and soothing.
- Refill visual: squeeze close to the gel surface and pull upward to create sharp “droplet peaks.”
- Droplet-peaks reference: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1k83hMzn7sRMYHCScMKRw6kp4_hzA2m0q/view?usp=sharing

Campaign-specific mandatory items:

- Before and after
- Full-size jar and refill
- Explain the combo value and uninterrupted routine
- Say “luxury” and “low-maintenance”
- Use “the Aesop of Vietnam” on camera or in caption
- Keep labels readable and not mirror-flipped
- Tag the correct TikTok and Instagram handles
- Include #cobasdaughter
- Tag the product in TikTok Shop or Amazon storefront

## Q4 gifting context

For Q4 bodycare gifting, use four strategic pillars: Gift Guide, Aesop Alternative, Keepsake, and Occasion. Show handwoven packaging as a functional object and a two-hand gifting gesture between two people.

Do not automatically add refill demonstrations, summer skin concerns, Amazon affiliate instructions, or seasonal refill techniques to a gifting brief unless the selected product and campaign require them.

Public output excludes internal revenue targets, AOV targets, and channel performance.
