### Objective

Introduce the Miniset as the small, considered body-care discovery a creator would genuinely keep on hand for a discerning friend. The recommendation must be earned through real product use before the creator calls it gift-worthy.

### Creator profile

- US-based, natural American English.
- Prefer 5K–50K followers with at least three comparable posts audited.
- Trusted beauty/body-care reviewer, monthly roundup creator, niche-beauty discoverer, or candid “products worth saving” voice.
- Comfortable naming who a product is and is not for.
- Avoid deal accounts, dupe-first creators, discount hauls, or creators with weak organic engagement.
- A likely rate below $200 is only a planning estimate until the creator submits an actual quote.

### Deliverable

- One 30–45 second vertical TikTok or Reel.
- Talking-head review with product cutaways or voice-over review with candid use.
- Product must be linked through the approved affiliate storefront or trackable product link.
- Creator must use the platform-required affiliate disclosure.
- Organic creator usage only unless paid media, whitelisting, or raw-footage rights are separately approved.

### Main idea

Show the small body-care ritual you are testing and saving before the noisy holiday gift-guide season. The reason to care is not a discount or fake urgency—it is the pleasure of finding a thoughtful, less-obvious Vietnamese body-care discovery early.

### Choose one opening route

- “A small body-care discovery I'm saving before the holiday gift guides get loud.”
- “For the friend who already knows every beauty brand: start here.”
- “I test body care before I recommend it as a gift—here's my verdict.”
- “The quiet-luxury mini ritual I would actually keep on hand.”

### Content sequence

1. **First frame:** face to camera with the full set, or a tight flat lay with the recipient tension written on screen.
2. **Discovery:** introduce CoBa's Daughter and its Vietnamese inspiration without generic “exotic” language.
3. **Test:** show one exfoliator and Aloe in use; give one honest observation about texture, ritual, or ease.
4. **Recipient logic:** name a type of woman, not a September occasion—for example, the beauty obsessive, frequent traveler, or quiet-luxury friend.
5. **Verdict:** explain why the compact three-step format is something you would save or keep on hand.
6. **CTA:** direct viewers to save the idea and open the linked product in your storefront.

### Must include

- Say **CoBa's Daughter** clearly on camera or in the voice-over.
- Position it as **Vietnamese-inspired, low-maintenance luxury body care**.
- Show the complete 3-Piece Set with readable, non-mirror-flipped labels.
- Show actual use before recommending it.
- Explain the recipient fit in one specific sentence.
- Keep the language early, save-worthy, and low pressure.
- Add the required affiliate disclosure and approved trackable link.

### Approval gates

The CoBa's Daughter mention, complete set, actual use, specific recipient, honest verdict, approved claims, affiliate disclosure, and trackable CTA must all be present.

### Do not include

- Hostess, occasion, Secret Santa, White Elephant, or Christmas language.
- Countdown, low-stock, “selling out,” or another unverified urgency claim.
- An unconfirmed price, promotion, bundle, or shortened set name.
- Scent-dupe or “smells like Aesop” language.
- Generic gift-guide listicles with no test or recipient logic.
